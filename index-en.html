Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add my clothes?

After signing in with Apple, tap "Add Item" in the Wardrobe tab to take a photo, pick one or several from your library, or paste a store product link. You can also choose Modus from the Share menu in Safari or a shopping app. The background is removed on your device with Apple Vision. Some stores don't share the product image in link previews; in that case, take a screenshot of the product and add it as a photo.

Where are my data and photos stored?

Modus has no servers of its own. Your wardrobe is stored on your device and in the private area of your own iCloud account; the developer cannot see your data. When you sign in on another iPhone with the same Apple Account, your wardrobe syncs automatically. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account and all my data?

Use "Delete Account & All Content" at the bottom of the Profile tab to permanently delete all wardrobe items, outfits and photos from your device and from iCloud.
